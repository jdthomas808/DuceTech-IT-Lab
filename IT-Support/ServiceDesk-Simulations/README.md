## Documentation Methodology

Each case study is structured to communicate:

1. **Ticket Overview** — The original request or reported issue.
2. **Technical Objective** — The intended troubleshooting or administrative outcome.
3. **Troubleshooting Workflow** — Recorded actions and applicable diagnostic procedures.
4. **Resolution Summary** — The verified outcome or available simulator feedback.
5. **Technical Explanation** — The underlying systems and technologies.
6. **Skills Demonstrated** — Relevant technical competencies.
7. **Lessons Learned** — Insights and opportunities for improvement.

Where original simulator transcripts or screenshots are unavailable, the case studies identify those limitations rather than presenting unverified actions as completed work.
