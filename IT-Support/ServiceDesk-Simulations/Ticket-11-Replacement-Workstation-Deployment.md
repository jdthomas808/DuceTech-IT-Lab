## 1. Ticket Overview

This service desk scenario involved preparing a replacement workstation for an employee.

Unlike incidents that can be resolved through remote troubleshooting, workstation replacement requires coordination between device preparation, configuration, and deployment.

The simulator included a PC Shelf and Ship Manager workflow for handling prepared computers.

## 5. Technical Explanation

### Workstation Imaging

Workstation imaging is a process used to prepare computers with an operating system, required software, and an approved configuration.

Organizations may use imaging or modern provisioning technologies to standardize endpoint deployment.

### Device Staging

Staging allows IT teams to prepare devices before assigning or delivering them to employees.

A staged workstation may require:

- Operating system configuration
- Driver installation
- Security updates
- Endpoint protection
- Required business applications
- Device inventory records
- User or department assignment

### Deployment and Shipping

Once a device is prepared and approved, the deployment process may involve assigning it to an employee, coordinating delivery, and recording asset information.

In this simulator, the PC Shelf represented the staging area, while Ship Manager represented the next step in the fulfillment workflow.

## 6. Troubleshooting Considerations

The simulator presented a situation where the expected workstation options were not available.

In a production environment, an IT technician would investigate:

- Whether a prepared device exists in inventory
- Whether the workstation has completed required configuration
- Whether the device is assigned to the correct request
- Whether additional preparation is required
- Whether the request should be transferred to an authorized deployment or inventory team

These are recommended operational considerations, not additional simulator actions claimed as completed.

## 9. Lessons Learned

Workstation replacement involves more than selecting a computer and sending it to an employee.

A successful deployment depends on device availability, proper configuration, accurate inventory tracking, and coordination between support and fulfillment processes.

This scenario also demonstrated the importance of understanding a service desk tool's workflow before attempting to complete a deployment request.

When expected equipment is unavailable, the technician should investigate the workflow and inventory status rather than assume that a replacement device is ready.
